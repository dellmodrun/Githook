THE Hatch WORKFLOW

Your next idea, from start to finish.

How it works
  1. 01

    Discover

    Tell your agent what you’re looking for. Find unfinished GitHub projects with evidence of work still to do.

  2. 02

    Research

    Compare demos, code activity, developer profiles and social evidence. Review the shortlist and choose your project.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Review the creator-reward split, then approve your Pump.fun launch with your wallet. Your coin gets its own Hatch page.

  4. 04

    Onboard

    Invite developers from info@hatchmind.fun with project context, their coin page and a guide to claiming eligible creator rewards.
