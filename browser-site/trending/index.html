How this ranking works

Coins in Hatch’s catalogue are sorted by reported USD volume in the selected rolling window, highest first. Each coin uses its most active matching SOL/USDC pool by 24-hour volume. This is a catalogue ranking, not every token on Solana.

Current rankings require successful observations less than 90 seconds old with positive measured volume. If none qualify, a clearly labeled last-observed ranking may use snapshots less than 15 minutes old. Older observations are excluded and unknown values remain blank. Market cap is never replaced with FDV. Volume can include bots or wash trading and is not a recommendation. Prices come from DexScreener, with GeckoTerminal as a fallback; on-chain curve checks are separate.
